Cinnabarr

Lets people browse and request shows and movies, manage downloads and play games from the Jellyfin apps. The address and API key stay on this server; the apps never see them.

How this Jellyfin server reaches Cinnabarr. Keep both on your home network.
In Cinnabarr: Settings → General → Security.

Who can do what

Administrators can do everything. Anyone not ticked here sees nothing from Cinnabarr. Request: browse, search and request. Manage: also downloads, interactive search and deleting. Play: games.
